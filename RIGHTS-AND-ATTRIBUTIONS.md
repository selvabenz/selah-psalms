## Literary analysis and metadata

The structure, parallelism, component, and alignment records are machine-proposed editorial drafts. Eight Psalms received individually authored pilot analysis; the remaining 142 received conservative systematic Hebrew drafts. They require human scholarly review.

Supplied scholarship informed the method, including Adele Berlin's “Parallelism” in *Anchor Bible Dictionary* 5 (1992), pp. 154–162, and Robert L. Alden's work on chiastic Psalms. Copies of those works are not included in this repository. Their mention does not license redistribution of the publications and does not attribute the project-specific judgments to those authors.
